# Punyakoti Taila — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name
Punyakoti Taila

Meaning:
- Punyakoti: Symbol of purity, honesty, and traditional Indian values
- Taila: Sanskrit/Kannada word for oil

Brand Positioning:
Premium organic cold-pressed oil brand rooted in Indian tradition, purity, and healthy living.

---

# Brand Vision

To bring back authentic traditional cold-pressed oils and natural products to every household while preserving purity, trust, and health.

---

# Brand Mission

Deliver high-quality, naturally extracted, chemical-free oils using traditional methods while maintaining honesty, sustainability, and customer trust.

# Brand Story

Punyakoti Taila is inspired by the timeless Indian tradition of purity, honesty, and naturally prepared food products.

Our oils are carefully extracted using traditional cold-pressed methods to preserve nutrients, aroma, and authentic taste.

We believe healthy living starts from the kitchen, and every drop of oil should carry purity, trust, and tradition.
