A golden drop held by two green leaves, encircled by a hand-painted ring suggesting a sunlit field. Pair it with the PUNYAKOTI / TAILA wordmark and the tagline "Pure tradition. Pure goodness." The mark is never re-drawn.

DO
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of one "P" of PUNYAKOTI around the lockup.
  • Use the seal version on labels, hang-tags, packing tape, and any stamp-like context.
  • At icon sizes under 32px, drop the ring and landscape — only drop + leaves remain.
  • Pair with batch / region stamps in mono (Manrope or DM Mono, 0.18em tracking).
DON'T
  • Don't re-draw the drop or leaves. Don't add stems, veins, or extra leaves.
  • Don't tilt or rotate the mark — keep it upright.
  • Don't ALL-CAPS the tagline larger than 12px; it loses its quietness.
  • Don't combine the mark with bright photographic backgrounds — use a solid panel beneath.
